01 / Why

Why Footprints Exists

"Every digital conversation leaves a footprint. Where does yours go?"

Today's AI sends your words to distant servers. They're stored, analysed, and sold. Footprints was built on a different belief: your conversations belong to you — and only you.

Your Message → Cloud Server → Processed → Stored ✕ Your Phone → You

No cloud. No intermediary. Privacy by architecture, not by policy.
